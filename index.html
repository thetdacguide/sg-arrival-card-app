SG Arrival Card App: Download and Submit on MyICA Mobile

If you would rather handle your Singapore arrival formalities from your phone, the official SG Arrival Card app is MyICA Mobile, the free app from Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA). There is no separate app called "SG Arrival Card" — MyICA Mobile is the app that contains the SG Arrival Card section, and it is the only official mobile way to submit your arrival card. Any app that charges a fee to file the card, or promises to do it for you, is not ICA.

How to download the SG Arrival Card app

Open the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for "MyICA Mobile". Before you install, check the seller: the listing should say the app is by the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority of Singapore. Do not download a paid lookalike with a similar name. The app is free to download and free to use. Once it is installed, open it and tap "SG Arrival Card" — that takes you straight to the SGAC section. Download the app before your travel day, not at the airport, so you are not setting it up in a queue.

What the app can do

Inside the app, the SG Arrival Card section lets you submit your arrival information and your electronic health declaration, scan your passport's biographical page instead of typing every detail, submit for a group of up to 10 travellers, and retrieve a record you already submitted.

How to submit on the app

Have your passport, flight details and Singapore accommodation address ready before you start. First, scan your passport. Then enter your trip details — arrival date, flight or other transport, and where you are staying. Then complete the health declaration. Review everything and submit within the 3-day window before arrival, including the arrival day itself (this guide to the SG arrival card 3-day rule explains how the window is counted). ICA then sends an acknowledgement email with your D/E number. Keep it: it is your proof of submission.

Can you download or print your card from the app?

In the MyICA app, you view your record — open the SGAC section and look under "Individual Submission" or "Group Submission". For a PDF copy, use the web e-Service: on the official SGAC e-Service you can download a PDF copy of your SGAC when you update your record. The full steps are in how to retrieve or download your SG Arrival Card. Either way, you do not need to print anything. Your submission is stored in ICA's system and linked to your passport, so at immigration you simply present your passport.

App or website: which should you use?

Neither is better — both lead to the same ICA system. Pick the app if you are travelling light and want the passport scan. Pick the website if you are on a computer and want a PDF copy saved there. Both are free, and both accept a group submission of up to 10 travellers.

What if the app is not working?

First, update MyICA Mobile to the latest version in your app store. If it still does not work, submit through the official web e-Service instead, at eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/. The 3-day window still applies, so switching channels is better than missing it.
